Daily planners · one per sport

Pick a day (3 days hour by hour, then a 5-day outlook) and you get a verdict, the best window, a chart that lines up every relevant variable on the same hour columns, and planning cards (gear, route wind, surface, what to wear, safety). The dashed frame marks the recommended window and dark columns mark night. Every screen lists its data sources at the bottom.

Weather accents · warm pastel

Colour is used only for things with a meaning. Text stays neutral. Each hue has one meaning everywhere: sage-teal = wind, apricot = heat, sea blue = water (rain, waves), honey = sun, lavender = storm and lightning, frost = snow and cold. Accents are pastel; warnings are the only strong colours.

Condition rating · 1 → 5

One hue, getting brighter as the rating improves. The number of filled dots also shows the rating, so it never depends on colour alone.
